How each finding is handled:

- **Institutional line payments don't reconcile** (outpatient, HHA, hospice; inpatient/SNF have no line payment): the claim header payment is authoritative and is placed on the first line. A reconciliation test proves per-claim totals match CMS.
- **Annual Part B / Part D / dual month totals disagree with monthly fields**: eligibility is always derived from the monthly fields, which line up with the claims (no carrier claims fall in Part-A-only months).
- **Enrollment years after March 2023**: the 2024-2025 files have no claims to match; they sit outside the reporting window.
- **DME claims whose line dates differ by a day**: claim dates are normalised to the claim-wide min/max so each claim has one incurred month.
- **No death dates**: mortality-based exclusions (e.g. readmission index stays ending in death) cannot be applied; noted as a limitation.
